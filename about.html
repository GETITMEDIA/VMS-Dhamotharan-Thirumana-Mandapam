Our Story

Built for the way South Indian families celebrate

VMS Dhamotharan Thirumana Mandapam stands on the Dindivanam Bypass Road, just beyond the Moratandi tollgate and directly opposite the Irumbai Sai Baba Temple. It is close enough to Pondicherry to be easy for your guests to reach, and far enough out to give you the space that a large function genuinely needs.

A traditional South Indian wedding is not one event â€” it is a day of them. Guests arrive through the morning, the ceremony runs on its own time, meals are served in rounds, and the family needs somewhere to change and rest between it all. This mandapam was planned around exactly that rhythm.

The main hall, the dining hall, the kitchen, the rooms and the parking are all on the same premises. Nothing has to be arranged elsewhere and nothing has to be moved between venues. The Experience

Everything you need for a memorable celebration

01

The Main Hall

A wide, brightly finished hall with a decorative false ceiling, a mezzanine level and full centralized air conditioning. It seats more than 500 guests without feeling crowded, and the open floor lets you set the stage and aisle wherever your ceremony needs them.

  • 500+ guest seating capacity
  • Full centralized air conditioning
  • Elegant interiors with decorative lighting
  • Open layout for flexible stage placement
02

Dining, Kitchen & Serving

A dining hall kept entirely separate from the main function area, with long stainless serving counters and seating for up to 250 guests at a time. Behind it, the kitchen and serving area is built to handle catering for as many as 1500 people â€” and the utensils for that scale are available on site.

  • Dining seating for 250 guests
  • Kitchen & serving capacity for up to 1500
  • Utensils available on the premises
  • Premium dedicated hand wash area
03

Comfort & Convenience

Rooms with attached bathrooms and air conditioning let the family rest and change on site, with a dedicated dressing room for the bride and groom and a separate room option when you need more space. Outside, there is open parking for cars and two-wheelers, and generator backup keeps the lights and the AC running whatever the grid does.

  • AC rooms with attached bathrooms
  • Dressing room & separate room option
  • Spacious car and bike parking
  • Generator power backup
