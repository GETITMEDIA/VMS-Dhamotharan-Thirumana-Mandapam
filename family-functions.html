The Occasion

One hall, arranged around your function

Not every family occasion comes with a fixed running order. A naming ceremony has its own rituals, a sixtieth wedding anniversary has quite different ones, and a family reunion has none at all beyond eating together and catching up. What they all need is a space that can be arranged around them rather than the other way round.

The hall takes gatherings from around fifty people up to the full 500-seat capacity. For a small function, seating is set out at one end and the rest left open; for a large one it opens out completely. The stage can carry a ritual, a lamp, an honouring ceremony or nothing at all, depending on what the day involves.

Because it is priced for real family budgets rather than as a wedding-only venue, a modest gathering does not end up paying wedding rates. Tell us what the function involves and we will help you work out how to lay the hall out for it.

Ways to Use the Hall

A few of the functions held here

01

Naming ceremonies and traditional functions

Naming ceremonies, ayush homam, valaikaappu, punyajanam and similar occasions all follow their own ritual order, usually in the morning, usually with the immediate family close in and a wider circle of relatives seated around. The open floor and level surface take a homam or lamp setup comfortably, and the air conditioning makes a long morning ceremony bearable for the mother, the child and the grandparents alike.

  • Level open floor suited to homam and lamp setups
  • Seating drawn in close for the immediate family
  • Air conditioned throughout the ceremony
  • Rooms on site to prepare and rest
02

Anniversaries, reunions and gatherings

Anniversaries and reunions are less about ritual and more about the meal and the time spent together. Here the dining hall does most of the work, running for 250 at a sitting while the main hall stays free for people to sit, talk and take photographs. Honouring ceremonies — elders receiving a shawl, a retirement being marked, grandchildren speaking — are easily held on the stage, and the hall stays comfortable well into the evening with generator backup covering the lighting.

  • Stage available for honouring ceremonies and speeches
  • Continuous dining while the hall stays open
  • Generator backup for evening functions
  • Budget-friendly for smaller family gatherings
