About the Mandapam

A grand setting for the moments your family will remember

VMS Dhamotharan Thirumana Mandapam sits just off the Dindivanam Bypass Road on the outskirts of Pondicherry, directly opposite the Irumbai Sai Baba Temple. Behind its temple-style facade is a wide, column-free hall that comfortably seats more than 500 guests under full centralized air conditioning.

Everything a large function needs is on the same premises — a separate dining hall, a kitchen and serving area built for large-scale catering, AC rooms with attached bathrooms, a dressing room for the family, generator backup and open parking for cars and two-wheelers.
