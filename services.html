In Detail

What each occasion looks like here

01

Weddings

A wedding fills a venue in a way nothing else does — the ceremony, the guests arriving through the morning, the meals in rounds, the family needing somewhere to change. The main hall seats over 500 guests under full centralized AC, and the open floor lets you place the manavarai wherever the ceremony calls for it. Dining runs separately for 250 at a time, so the hall never has to be cleared mid-function.

  • 500+ seating in the air-conditioned main hall
  • Separate dining hall for 250 guests per sitting
  • Dressing room for the bride and groom
  • AC rooms with attached bathrooms for the family

02

Receptions

Receptions are about flow — a stage everyone can see, an aisle for guests to come up and greet the couple, and space at the back that does not turn into a bottleneck. The hall's decorative false ceiling and lighting photograph well in the evening, and the mezzanine level gives photographers a clear vantage over the whole room.

  • Wide stage area with clear sightlines
  • Decorative ceiling lighting for evening functions
  • Mezzanine level for photography
  • Generator backup so the evening never pauses

Enquire About a Reception

03

Birthday Celebrations

A first birthday or a milestone one, the hall scales down as comfortably as it scales up. Set out only the seating you need and keep the rest of the floor open for children to move around — the polished floor and full air conditioning make a long afternoon celebration genuinely comfortable for older relatives too.

  • Flexible seating for small or large gatherings
  • Full centralized air conditioning
  • Separate dining hall for the meal
  • Open parking for arriving guests

Enquire About a Birthday

04

Kaathukuthal

An ear-piercing ceremony brings the whole extended family together for a morning of ritual followed by a proper meal. The hall gives you room for the ceremony itself and the relatives who come to watch it, and the dining hall next door means the sadhya can be served without anyone waiting outside.

  • Traditional ceremony space with room for relatives
  • Dining hall for 250 guests at a sitting
  • Utensils available for large-scale catering
  • Rooms on site for the family to prepare

Enquire About a Kaathukuthal

05

Engagements

An engagement is usually a smaller, warmer affair than the wedding that follows — both families, the formal exchange, and a meal together. Use a portion of the hall and keep it intimate, or open it out if both sides are bringing everyone. Either way the same facilities are available, and it is a natural chance to see the venue before booking the wedding itself.

  • Scalable layout for an intimate or large gathering
  • Air-conditioned interiors throughout
  • Separate rooms for each family
  • Dining arrangements for both sides

Enquire About an Engagement

06

Family Functions

Housewarmings, naming ceremonies, retirements, reunions, memorial gatherings — the occasions that do not have a standard template but still need a proper space, a proper meal and somewhere for elders to sit comfortably. Tell us what the function involves and we will help you work out how to lay the hall out for it.

  • Suitable for traditional and private occasions
  • Premium hand wash area and clean facilities
  • Car and two-wheeler parking on the premises
  • Budget-friendly for smaller functions too
