Occasions We Host

Six kinds of celebration, one venue

The same facilities serve all of them — the hall, the dining area, the rooms and the parking are yours for the day regardless of the occasion. Open any occasion below to see how the venue is arranged for it.

01

Wedding

Muhurtham at first light, relatives arriving through the morning, and lunch running in sittings until the last one has eaten. The main hall seats over 500 under full air conditioning with no pillars in the way, so the manavarai goes wherever the ceremony calls for it.

  • 500+ seats in one pillar-free span
  • Separate dining for 250 a sitting
  • Rooms for the bride, groom and family

Learn More

02

Reception

A reception lives on flow — a stage everyone can see, and a greeting line that keeps moving. Steps on both sides send guests up one way and down the other, and the illuminated false ceiling gives the evening its warmth without extra rigging.

  • Wide stage with clear sightlines
  • Decorative ceiling lighting for evenings
  • Mezzanine vantage for photography

Learn More

03

Birthday Celebrations

A first birthday with sixty relatives or a sixtieth with three hundred — the hall scales down as comfortably as it scales up. Set out only the seating you need and leave the rest of the polished floor open for the children.

  • Flexible layout for any size gathering
  • Safe indoor space away from the road
  • Air conditioned through a long afternoon

Learn More

04

Kaathukuthal

The ear-piercing itself takes minutes; the relatives who travel for it, the blessings from the elders and the meal afterwards take the whole morning. Seating is arranged around the ritual rather than in rows, so everyone can actually see it.

  • Ritual space with family gathered close
  • Traditional sadhya for 250 a sitting
  • Comfortable seating for elders

Learn More

05

Engagement

The nichayathartham is smaller and warmer than the wedding that follows — both families, the thattu of gifts, the rings, and the wedding date read out to everyone at once. Use a portion of the hall or open it right out.

  • Decorated stage for the ring exchange
  • A separate room for each family
  • Intimate or full 500-seat scale

Learn More

06

Family Functions

Naming ceremonies, anniversaries, housewarmings, reunions, retirements — occasions with no standard template that still need a proper space, a proper meal and somewhere for elders to sit comfortably.

  • Gatherings from fifty to five hundred
  • Suits homam, lamp and honouring setups
  • Priced for real family budgets

How Booking Works

Four simple steps to your date

No complicated process — a phone call and a visit are usually all it takes.

  1. 01

    Send your enquiry

    Call us or send your details on WhatsApp with your event type and the date you have in mind.

  2. 02

    Check availability

    We confirm whether your date is free and talk through what your function will need.

  3. 03

    Visit the mandapam

    Come and see the hall, dining area and rooms in person before you decide anything.

  4. 04

    Confirm & celebrate

    Reserve your date, and we make sure the venue is ready for your family on the day.
